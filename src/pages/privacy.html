Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information submitted through this website and our consultation process is handled.

Information collected

Booking and enquiry systems may collect contact details, property information, floor plans, photographs and — only where the optional Astro‑Vastu service is commissioned — birth information.

Use

Information should be used only to deliver the requested consultation, administer bookings, process payments, provide requested communications and meet legal obligations.

Marketing

Marketing consent should be optional and unticked by default. Every marketing communication must include a working unsubscribe method.

Storage

Sensitive property and personal information should be stored in restricted-access systems, never placed in public URLs, and retained only as long as necessary.
