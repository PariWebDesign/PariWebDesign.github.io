Terms & Cancellation

These terms explain the current booking, credit, rescheduling and service-scope rules for Australian clients.

Diagnostic Session

The A$249 Diagnostic is a 60-minute online consultation for one property, including limited floor-plan pre-review, three written priorities and a recommended next step.

Credits

The A$249 Diagnostic fee may be credited toward an eligible Blueprint purchased within 30 days. The A$29 Discovery credit and A$249 Diagnostic credit do not stack.

Rescheduling

Working policy: one no-fee reschedule when requested at least 24 hours before the appointment, subject to final legal review.

Site visits

Australian site visits are not included in online consultation fees and are quoted separately.
